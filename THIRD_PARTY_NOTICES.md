# Third-party material

The root MIT license covers the original Blue Hour application code and original teaching material. It does not relicense third-party datasets, compositions, recordings, packages, or retained vendor code.

- **ChoCo chord annotations**: selected iReal community annotations from ChoCo by Jacopo de Berardinis, Albert Meroño-Peñuela, Andrea Poltronieri and Valentina Presutti. Used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The exported dataset retains attribution, a pinned source commit, per-item SHA-256 values and conversion notes. See [repertoire provenance](public/REPERTOIRE-LICENSES.txt), [catalog metadata](lib/standard-catalog.ts) and the [source license statement](https://github.com/smashub/choco/blob/5fe168fd55be5c84512abcfbc4e6f1b1f8f0092a/LICENSE.md).
- **Four sourced melody arrangements**: Freed Jazz source scores and their stated United States public-domain status, with the app's adaptation notes, are documented individually in [REPERTOIRE-LICENSES.txt](public/REPERTOIRE-LICENSES.txt). This is not a statement that every jazz standard is public domain worldwide.
- **Master recordings**: official listening/release links only. No commercial recordings, complete Real Book scans, lyrics, or artist solo transcriptions are distributed.
- **Retained Sites integration**: preserve [build/sites-vite-plugin.LICENSE](build/sites-vite-plugin.LICENSE).
- **Retained shadcn stylesheet**: preserve [vendor/shadcn-tailwind-4.13.0.LICENSE.md](vendor/shadcn-tailwind-4.13.0.LICENSE.md).
- **Dependencies**: third-party npm packages keep their respective licenses. Exact installed versions are captured in pnpm-lock.yaml.

Dataset licensing and software licensing do not transfer rights in the underlying compositions or recordings. Preserve source attributions when redistributing extracted charts.
